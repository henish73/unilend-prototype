UniLend

Dry-run video plan

The brief asks for one unedited recording of at most 3 minutes, showing a team member or proxy doing the tasks with the proposed script. A person has to record this. It can't be generated.

Before recording

  • ☐ Prototype and variant are frozen, with rewards set and lockVariant: true in prototype/js/config.js
  • ☐ Proxy lined up, ideally someone who did not build the prototype
  • ☐ Phone charged, on Do Not Disturb, with screen recording and microphone on
  • ☐ Two timed rehearsals done, aiming for about 2:30
  • ☐ Facilitator tools page opened, and all three "Reset and start" links work on the phone

Run order

Target is about 2:30, with 3:00 as the hard limit.

TimeWhoWhat
0:00FacilitatorReads the opening (below)
0:15FacilitatorReads Task 1. Proxy works with no hints.
1:05FacilitatorResets to Task 2, reads Task 2. Proxy works with no hints.
1:50FacilitatorResets to Task 3, reads Task 3. Proxy works with no hints.
2:35FacilitatorReads the close

Sign-in is inside Task 1, so Task 1 takes a little longer than the others.

Words to read

Opening: "We are evaluating the design. Any difficulties help us improve it. You may pause or stop at any time. Please describe what you are looking for and expecting. The rewards shown in the app are fictional, and no real prizes are given."

Task 1: "It's 2 p.m., your phone is at 5% and you have a lab at 3. Get a charger from another student on campus."

Task 2: "You borrowed a charger yesterday and are done with it. Give it back."

Task 3: "A classmate has asked to borrow your scientific calculator. Help them out. Then see what your help has earned you and do something with it."

Close: "That's the end of the tasks. Thank you."

Rules

  • The facilitator gives no hints. If the proxy is stuck, the only allowed prompts are "What are you looking for?" and "What would you do next?"
  • If it goes wrong, record the whole take again. Editing is not allowed.
  • Leave the failure switches on the facilitator page off.
  • In Task 2, stop timing at "Return confirmed", but say nothing and let the proxy carry on until they say they are done.

Afterwards

  • ☐ Check that the video is under 3:00 and shows the frozen variant
  • ☐ Do not trim it or add cuts
