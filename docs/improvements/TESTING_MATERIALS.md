# UniLend: testing materials (print-ready)

## Research questions and metrics

| Task | Research question | Metrics and targets |
|---|---|---|
| T1 Borrow | Can a student in a hurry arrange and confirm a pickup with a classmate without help? | Unaided completion 8/10 or more · median time under 3 min · 1 or fewer wrong codes per person |
| T2 Return | After returning, do students notice and act on Pass-It-On without being told? | Unaided completion 9/10 or more · Pass-It-On outcome counts |
| T3a Lend | Can a lender accept a request and hand over an item without help? | Unaided completion 8/10 or more · median time under 2 min |
| T3b Rewards | Do lenders find what their help earned without being pointed to it? | Found Rewards unaided 7/10 or more · rewards claimed (count) |
| All | How hard did each task feel? | SEQ median 5.5 or higher out of 7 |

**Definitions:**
- **Unaided:** no hint.
- **Assisted:** any hint was given (record it word for word).
- **Not completed:** the participant gave up or reached the time cap.
- **Off-route tap:** opening a screen that is not on the shortest route.

**Time caps:** T1 5 min; T2, T3a and T3b 3 min.

## Task cards (read word for word)

**T1.** It's 2 p.m. and you're on campus. Your phone is at 5%, you left your charger at home, and your lab starts at 3. You'd rather not leave campus to buy one.

**T2.** Yesterday you borrowed a USB-C charger from a classmate through UniLend. You've finished with it, and you're now at the library where you picked it up.

**T3a.** A classmate has asked on UniLend to borrow a scientific calculator. You have one in your bag and won't need it until tomorrow.

**T3b.** You've heard that helping people on UniLend gets you something. You're curious what.

## Facilitator script

- **Opening:** "Thanks for coming. We're testing the app, not you. If something is hard, that's useful for us. You can stop or skip anything at any time. The rewards in the app are made up. Is it OK if we record the screen and your voice?"
- **Think-aloud:** "While you use the app, please say out loud what you're looking at, what you're trying to do and what you expect to happen. Let's practise: open your phone's settings and tell me out loud how you'd change the screen brightness." (Allow about 30 seconds. Don't correct them.)
- **Each task:** "I'll read a situation. Do whatever you'd normally do, and tell me when you're done." Read the card, start the timer and hand over the phone.
- **During:** use only "Please keep talking", "What are you looking for?", "What did you expect that to do?" and "What would you do next?"
- **After each task:** ask the SEQ, then one screen-specific question while showing that screen again (for example, "On this screen, what was the code for?").
- **Close:** "Thank you. Please don't describe the app to others until testing is over today."

## Pre-test questions

1. Program and year.
2. How comfortable are you using new phone apps? (1 Not at all comfortable · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 Very comfortable)
3. Tell me about the last time you needed something on campus that you didn't have with you. What did you do?
4. This term, how often have you **lent** something to another student? (Never · Once · 2-3 times · About monthly · Weekly or more)
5. This term, how often have you **borrowed** something from another student? (same options)
6. How often do you use apps or group chats to borrow, swap or give things away? (Never · Rarely · Sometimes · Often · Very often)

## After each task (SEQ)

"Overall, how difficult or easy was this task?" (1 Very difficult · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 6 · 7 Very easy)

## Post-test questions

1. "Seeing the points made me want to help others." (1 Strongly disagree · 2 Disagree · 3 Neutral · 4 Agree · 5 Strongly agree) Why?
2. (Show the Pass-It-On screen.) "What was this screen asking you to do? Why did you choose what you chose?"
3. Which moment, if any, was confusing? (Show that screen.)
4. Compared with the last time you needed something on campus, how would this be better or worse?
5. When you decide whether to lend something to a classmate, what matters to you?
6. Anything else?
