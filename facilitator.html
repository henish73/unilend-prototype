Task 1: Borrow an item

"It's 2 p.m., your phone is at 5% and you have a lab at 3. Get a charger from another student on campus."

Starts at sign-in. Success with no hints: a pickup spot is chosen and the hand-off is confirmed. Limit: 3 minutes.

Task 2: Return an item and see what follows

"You borrowed a charger yesterday and are done with it. Give it back."

Starts at home with a charger on loan. Success with no hints: the return is confirmed. Stop the timer at "Return confirmed", then stay silent and keep watching until the participant says they are done. Record Pass-It-On as taken, declined or not seen. Limit: 3 minutes.

Reset and start Task 2

Task 3: Lend an item and look at what you have earned

"A classmate has asked to borrow your scientific calculator. Help them out. Then see what your help has earned you and do something with it."

Starts at home with Sam's request waiting. Success with no hints: the request is accepted, the hand-off is confirmed, and one claim is completed. Limit: 3 minutes.

Reset and start Task 3

Session log on this phone

The app records each task run on the phone it runs on: time from Begin (or from opening a reset link) to the task's confirmation, taps, backtracks, recovery states and Pass-It-On. It supports the note-taker's sheet and does not replace it. Export it from the phone the participants used.

Tip: on the participant's phone, press and hold the UniLend name for about a second to open a quick panel. Choose the participant and task, read the scenario, then tap Begin.
